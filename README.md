# Constella ✦

**A sky of feelings.** Every emotion you log becomes a star in your own night sky —
and the stars you plant in the same week reach for each other and become a named
constellation: the shape your days drew together.

Built with **Expo SDK 57** — runs entirely inside **Expo Go**. No accounts, no cloud:
your whole sky lives on your device.

---

## Run it

You need [Node.js](https://nodejs.org) and the **Expo Go** app on your phone
(iOS / Android — make sure it's up to date).

```bash
git clone https://github.com/rebecca002123/Expo-app.git
cd Expo-app
npm install
npx expo start
```

Then scan the QR code with **Expo Go** and look up. ✦

> If your PC and phone are on different networks, use `npx expo start --tunnel`.

## What's inside

- **Stars, not entries** — tap **＋**, pick one of six moods, add a few words.
  Each mood burns as a different star: Happy is a warm five-point sun, Peaceful a
  still pale-blue orb, Anxious a thin six-point star that flickers fast but never
  goes out, Inspired a violet six-ray that makes the sky lean in.
- **Words become brightness** — a tiny on-device sentiment reader listens to the
  tone of what you wrote. Light words burn brighter; heavy words burn dimmer but
  still hold their place. Nothing ever leaves your phone.
- **Weekly constellations** — stars planted in the same week are joined by faint
  dashed lines, in the order the feelings arrived, and the week names itself:
  *The Quiet Lantern*, *The Brave River*, *The Silver Kite*…
- **A living night** — the sky's gradient breathes with your clock (dawn, dusk,
  deep night), the moon in the corner shows tonight's **real phase**, dust stars
  keep the dark company, and occasionally something falls. Make a wish.
- **Deterministic placement** — every star's position and rhythm are seeded from
  its own id, so your sky is unique but never rearranges itself.
- **Revisit or release** — tap any star to reread the night it was born, its
  keywords and tone — or choose *Let it fade*.
- **Gentle by design** — honors your system Reduce Motion setting.

## Lineage

Constella carries forward the soul of MoodScape (feelings that become procedural,
living things; on-device sentiment; a sky that follows your clock) into a new
world: instead of islands in an endless sky, your emotions become the sky itself.
